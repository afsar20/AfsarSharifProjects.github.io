About these projects

I work in equity market structure and execution analytics in London. My interests include where liquidity is available, how trading costs affect results, and how portfolios behave when markets change.

These are personal research projects. The pages identify whether the data are public, collected through a broker, or simulated. The code and methods are available for review.
