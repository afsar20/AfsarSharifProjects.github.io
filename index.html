Working method

Research that can survive scrutiny

I keep the analysis traceable from the source through to the conclusion, and separate what the data shows from what needs a deeper execution-level investigation.

01

Frame the decision

Start with the liquidity, execution or product question rather than a list of available fields.

02

Validate the source

Make schema changes, missing mappings and reconciliation failures visible before analysing.

03

Test the signal

Use comparable baselines and simple statistics that a technical or commercial audience can challenge.

04

State the limits

Distinguish a useful monitoring signal from a causal execution-quality conclusion.
